# Wisdom Circle Story Engine

**All-ages collaborative story creation for connection, imagination, and shared wisdom.**

Wisdom Circle is a gentle, local-first story engine that helps people of every age co-create meaningful stories. It draws on timeless human themes — curiosity, kindness, courage, belonging, wonder, and repair — while remaining fully respectful of diverse worldviews and inner experiences.

> **Important Note**  
> This is a creative companion for storytelling, reflection, and gentle connection. It is **not** therapy, diagnosis, medical advice, or crisis support.  
> Stories can surface feelings. If you or someone you know is in distress, please reach out to trusted people or professional resources (e.g., 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline in the US). Pause and seek support whenever needed.

## Why it exists

Stories are one of the oldest ways humans make sense of life, pass on values, feel less alone, and awaken to what matters. Wisdom Circle gives families, classrooms, friends, elders, youth, and individuals a simple, pressure-free space to build those stories together — without ads, tracking, algorithms that push performance, or forced conclusions.

## Core Features (MVP Vision)

- **Age-adaptive prompts** — gentle guidance that grows with the tellers (child, teen, adult, elder). Prompts invite rather than instruct.
- **Shared circle mode** — take turns adding lines, images, choices, or silence. Consent and pacing are explicit.
- **Wisdom seeds** — optional starting themes drawn from universal human experiences (friendship, wonder, repair, gratitude, belonging, courage) rather than any single doctrine or ideology.
- **Local-first & data sovereignty** — stories stay on your device by default. No cloud required for core use. Export and share only when you choose.
- **Export** — plain text, Markdown, or printable storybook format. Future: gentle PDF with optional simple illustrations.
- **Accessibility first** — screen-reader friendly, high-contrast options, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, large-text friendly, multilingual-ready scaffolding.

## Getting Started

This repository is currently in early scaffolding — a living invitation rather than a finished product.

1. Clone the repo  
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/irecov3r3d/Wisdom-Circle-story-engine.git
   cd Wisdom-Circle-story-engine
   ```
2. The project is designed to run with minimal dependencies. Future iterations will include a simple HTML/JS interface that opens directly in the browser (no build step required).
3. Until the interface arrives, use the Wisdom Seeds below as starting points for offline or paper-based circles.

### Example Wisdom Seed
**Seed: The Light We Carry**  
One person begins: “In a quiet place, someone found a small light that only grew when shared…”  
Each person adds one sentence, one feeling, or one question. End whenever the circle feels complete. No wrong endings.

## Design Principles

- **Safety** — no dark patterns, no surprise content, clear consent for sharing or continuing. Emotional safety is as important as technical safety. Prompts avoid trauma triggers by default; users can opt into deeper themes.
- **Clarity** — every interaction explains what happens next. Language stays plain and invitational.
- **Access** — usable on low-end devices, offline-capable, multilingual-ready, works with assistive tech and mixed abilities.
- **Reach** — works for mixed-age groups, solo reflection, classrooms, and quiet evenings alike.
- **Respect** — themes are offered as invitations, never requirements. Diverse worldviews and ways of knowing are welcomed without ranking.
